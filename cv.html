01 / RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

Research Experience

Graduate Student Researcher · Lupo Lab June 2025 – Present

University of California, San Francisco · Department of Radiology and Biomedical Imaging

Computational analysis of longitudinal multiparametric brain MRI, radiopathomic maps, and clinical outcomes in glioblastoma.

  • Designed longitudinal feature-extraction strategies across pre-radiotherapy, post-radiotherapy, pre-progression, and progression imaging visits; characterized ROI distributions, lesion volumes, and within-patient changes.
  • Analyzed cellularity and tumor-probability maps using region-level statistics and threshold-based high-region quantification, including morphology-based cleaning and volume measurements.
  • Adapted MRI preprocessing and quality-control workflows addressing segmentation, registration/alignment, feature normalization, and incomplete longitudinal observations.
  • Conducted Kaplan–Meier and multivariable Cox regression analyses, including clinical covariate adjustment and multiple-testing considerations; prepared publication-quality figures and manuscript materials.

First-author manuscript in preparation.

BAYESIAN INFERENCE · MRI FEATURES

Bayesian Modeling of Treatment Response ↗

Used the I-SPY2 imaging cohort to examine pathologic complete response through Bayesian logistic regression with MRI-derived diameter and sphericity plus HR/HER2 status. Compared model specifications using leave-one-out predictive criteria and interpreted posterior odds ratios and uncertainty intervals.

DIMENSIONALITY REDUCTION · CLASSIFICATION

Breast Tumor Classification with PCA & QDA ↗

Applied principal component analysis and quadratic discriminant analysis to breast tumor morphology measurements. The reported project retained ten principal components and achieved 93.9% test accuracy and 0.991 ROC-AUC, with attention to class-level diagnostic errors.
