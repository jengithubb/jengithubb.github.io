Selected projects.

A collection of projects spanning biomedical images, statistical modeling, and clinical data analysis.

01 / MEDICAL IMAGING

Radiopathomic Pathology Maps for Glioblastoma MRI Analysis

Analyzed pathology-informed maps derived from multiparametric brain MRI in patients with newly diagnosed glioblastoma. The workflow included NIfTI imaging, tumor-region visualization, radiopathomic feature extraction, and associations with clinical outcomes.

  • Inspected contrast-enhancing and non-enhancing lesions and pathology-informed overlays with 3D Slicer.
  • Extracted imaging-derived features across longitudinal timepoints.
  • Examined relationships with progression-free and overall survival using statistical methods.
PythonNiBabel3D SlicerSurvival Analysis
02 / MACHINE LEARNING

Blood Cell Image Classification with Machine Learning and Deep Learning

Classified eight types of blood cells using the BloodMNIST dataset. Compared a random forest baseline with a compact PyTorch convolutional neural network using class-weighted loss for imbalanced categories.

  • Applied preprocessing, model training, and multi-class evaluation.
  • Evaluated accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and one-vs-rest AUC.
  • Achieved approximately 90.1% test accuracy with the CNN.
PyTorchscikit-learnMedMNIST
03 / CLINICAL DATA

Hormone Therapy and Breast Cancer Recurrence

Investigated hormone therapy and recurrence-free survival among estrogen receptor-positive breast cancer patients in the METABRIC dataset.

  • Fit adjusted, unadjusted, and stratified Cox proportional hazards models.
  • Used multiple imputation and assessed the proportional hazards assumption.
  • Observed a nonsignificant trend toward lower recurrence hazard after adjustment.
RMETABRICCox RegressionMultiple Imputation
04 / BAYESIAN STATISTICS

Bayesian Modeling of Breast Cancer Treatment Response

Explored whether baseline MRI tumor features and molecular subtype characteristics were associated with pathologic complete response.

  • Fit Bayesian logistic regression models in R using brms.
  • Checked convergence and posterior predictions and compared models via leave-one-out cross-validation.
  • Found stronger associations for receptor and HER2 status than for baseline tumor size or sphericity.
RbrmsMCMCBreast MRI
05 / CLASSIFICATION

Breast Tumor Classification with PCA and QDA

Applied dimensionality reduction and Quadratic Discriminant Analysis to the Breast Cancer Wisconsin dataset.

  • Studied 569 observations and 30 numeric predictors.
  • Retained 10 principal components representing more than 95% of variance.
  • Achieved 93.9% accuracy and 0.991 ROC AUC.
PythonPCAQDAROC AUC
06 / MACHINE LEARNING

Brain Tumor Classification Using Machine Learning

Compared logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost for classification using clinical and diagnostic variables.

  • Examined predictors including demographics, tumor features, symptoms, and imaging-related indicators.
  • Used sensitivity and specificity to assess classification performance.
  • Discussed weak overall predictive performance and the importance of data quality and cautious clinical interpretation.
