01 / ABOUT

Science driven by curiosity.

I work at the intersection of biomedical imaging, statistics, and computation to explore questions in human health.

I recently completed my M.S. in Health Data Science at UCSF and currently conduct research in the Lupo Lab, where I work with longitudinal brain MRI and clinical data in glioblastoma research.

I enjoy translating complex datasets into clear analytical workflows—from image processing and feature extraction to statistical modeling and scientific visualization. I'm also interested in emerging applications of neural signal analysis and brain–computer interfaces.
