01 / ARCHITECTURE BENCHMARKING

Neural Network Model Run-off

Implemented and compared a two-hidden-layer dense network, a deeper multilayer network, and a residual dense network in TensorFlow/Keras for a synthetic binary classification task (1,000 observations; 20 features).

Applied standardized features, five-fold cross-validation, early stopping, dropout, batch normalization, and comparison of predictive performance versus model complexity.

02 / HEALTHCARE NLP

LLM & Prompting Exercises

Explored language-model inference and healthcare question answering with Hugging Face APIs/local model interfaces. Constructed zero-shot, one-shot, and few-shot prompt templates and experimented with simple response-evaluation criteria.

These activities were course exercises rather than a validated clinical chatbot or production inference system.

03 / TRANSFORMER FOUNDATIONS

Exploring GPT-Style Architecture

Studied a guided nanoGPT implementation covering token and positional embeddings, masked multi-head self-attention, feed-forward blocks, residual connections, LayerNorm, autoregressive generation, and a training/evaluation workflow.

Focus: understanding architectural components and how attention-based language models are constructed. A completed nanoGPT training run is not claimed.

04 / CONNECTION TO BIOMEDICAL RESEARCH

Research Perspective

These exercises broadened my exposure to modern deep learning and language-model methods alongside my applied quantitative imaging and biostatistics background. I am interested in their potential applications to biomedical data and computational neuroscience.
