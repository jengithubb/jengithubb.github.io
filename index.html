02 / ABOUT & RESEARCH DIRECTION

A statistical foundation. A biomedical focus.

I use computational and statistical methods to connect biomedical measurements with questions that matter in research and patient care.

I completed an M.S. in Health Data Science at UCSF following undergraduate training in statistics. My work spans biomedical image processing, longitudinal data analysis, model evaluation, and scientific communication.

I’m especially interested in future research combining medical imaging, machine learning, and neuroscience. I am exploring neural signal analysis and brain–computer interfaces as developing interests, alongside my current imaging research.
