02 / ABOUT & RESEARCH DIRECTION

A rigorous foundation. An open research horizon.

I bring statistical reasoning and computational workflows to biomedical problems, from images and longitudinal measurements to interpretable evidence.

I completed an M.S. in Health Data Science at UCSF after undergraduate training in statistics. My experience combines quantitative imaging, statistical inference, applied machine learning projects, reproducible code, and communicating results through scientific figures.

My broader interests include medical imaging, machine learning for biomedical data, computational bioengineering, and neuroscience. I am building foundational knowledge in neural signal processing and brain–computer interfaces as future research directions.
